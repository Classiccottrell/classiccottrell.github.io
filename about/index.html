About

Matthew A. Cottrell

I take complicated software apart, then put it back simpler.

I’m a product designer. For seven years I’ve designed enterprise IT software, the kind of tool people use all day at work and only notice when it slows them down. I like the complicated parts. I take them apart until they make sense, then put them back together with fewer steps and clearer status. Before that I spent six years in creative services, designing campaigns for adidas, the NBA and E.D. Smith.

Outside the day job I build the tools I wish I’d had: VPAT Vault for accessibility reports, linefield for generative backgrounds, Forma for 3D renders, and an agent bench that runs my own projects. I’m also building Terry Time, a shop for Terry, the face that keeps turning up on East Van walls. I write about design and AI on Substack, and I draw ink portraits of film characters who stuck with me.
